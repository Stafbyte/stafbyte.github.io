Street Kings: Idle Tycoon

From the corner to the crown

Build your block, run your operations, hire a crew of characters and watch the cash roll in, even while you are away. A cartoon idle tycoon with a story, events and a street that never stops growing.

Free to play. Optional ads and purchases. No account needed.

What you get

Build and automate

Open new operations, upgrade them, hire managers and automate the whole block so it keeps earning while you are offline.

Collect your crew

Dozens of characters to find, level up and dress. Card packs and crates reward the way you play.

Chapters without end

A story across many chapters, then endless ones that keep getting tougher. Every chapter is a fresh climb from the bottom.

Events all year

Rotating and seasonal events open a separate park with its own levels, start cash and leaderboard tiers.

Fair and optional

Every ad is your choice and the game is fully playable without paying. Time skips credit your earnings as if the hours had passed.

Play your way

Short check-ins or long sessions. Your progress is saved on your device and there is nothing to sign up for.
