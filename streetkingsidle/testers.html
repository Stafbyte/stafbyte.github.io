Become a tester

Street Kings: Idle Tycoon · Android · free

Street Kings is a cartoon idle tycoon game coming to Google Play. Before it launches, Google requires a test with real players. If you have an Android phone and a Gmail account, you can help, and you will get to play it before everyone else.

What we are asking

  • Join the test and keep the game installed for 14 days.
  • Open it now and then. A couple of minutes a few times a week is plenty.
  • Tell us about anything that looks broken. Feedback is welcome but not required.

In this test version, ads and purchases are test versions only. Nothing real is charged.

How to join

  1. Join the tester group. Open the Street Kings Testers group on your phone or computer, signed in to the Google account you use on your Android phone, and click Join group. Your address stays private; other members cannot see it.
  2. Open the opt-in link. Open the opt-in page on your phone, signed in to the same Google account, and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install from Google Play. After you have joined, install the game from Google Play. It can take a few minutes to show up after you opt in.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days and open it occasionally. That is all.

Your privacy

Google Groups only uses your Google account address to add you to the test list. We do not share or sell it. The game itself has no accounts and keeps its saves on your device. See the privacy policy.
