Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Pixel Sudoku ("the app", "the game") is made by Stafbyte ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We have written it to meet the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Google Play User Data policy.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your progress, settings, statistics and card collection are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects some device information, including your Android advertising ID. We do not receive that information.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (current puzzle, scores, times, medals, cards, postcards, settings and the "Remove Ads" status) on your device. This stays on your device. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. "Reset Statistics" in the app deletes your statistics.

Information collected by advertising partners

The free version of the app shows ads using Google AdMob (Google LLC). When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and general (approximate) location derived from the IP address;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interaction and diagnostic information, such as whether an ad was shown, clicked or failed to load.

Google uses this to deliver, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google's use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your device settings and where you live.

If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops requesting ads.

Your choices about ads

  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads (the exact menu name depends on your device).
  • You can delete your advertising ID in the same place.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent form before personalised ads are requested.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and billing information under its own privacy policy. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase succeeded, so that it can turn ads off. If you reinstall the app or use a new device, use "Restore purchase" in the Me tab.

Information we do not collect

We do not run our own analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services in the app. We do not collect contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, health or financial information. We do not use a server for your game data.

Children

The app is a general audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

Overseas disclosure

Google processes advertising and purchase data in countries around the world, including the United States and Australia. By using the free, ad-supported version you accept that Google may handle that information outside Australia under its own policies.

Security and retention

We keep nothing on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to secure or delete there. Information held by Google is kept under Google's policies.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Because we hold none, the answer will normally be "none". To manage the data Google holds, use your Google account's privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com/.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a privacy matter you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (https://www.oaic.gov.au/).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version in the app and on our Google Play listing.
