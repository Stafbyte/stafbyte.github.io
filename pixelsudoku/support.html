Support

Need help? Email support@stafbyte.com. Tell us your device, Android version and what happened.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There are no accounts. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the progress is gone and we cannot restore it.

Do I have to watch ads?

The game is free and supported by ads. A short ad can appear between games, and you can watch optional video ads for extras. You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase.

I bought Remove Ads on another phone

Open the Me tab and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Google account.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. You can request one from your Play purchase history.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. Clear the game's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. See the Privacy Policy for what Google collects for ads.

Where can I report a bug?

Email us. Screenshots and your device model help a lot.
