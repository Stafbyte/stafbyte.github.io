Pixel Sudoku

Killer sudoku in pixel art

Pixel Sudoku is a cosy pixel-art take on Killer Sudoku. Fill the grid so every row, column and 3x3 box holds 1 to 9, and every dashed cage adds up to the small number in its corner. No digit repeats inside a cage.
