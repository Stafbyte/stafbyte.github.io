About ads

Pixel Sudoku is free to play and is supported by ads. Ads are part of the game.

What you will see

  • A short ad can appear between games.
  • Optional video ads: watch one to get an extra hint or a second chance after a game over.

How ads work

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google collects some information from your device, including your advertising ID. We do not receive that information. See the Privacy Policy for details and for how to reset or opt out of ad personalisation.

Remove ads

You can remove ads for good with a one-time purchase of 10 Australian dollars (or the local price shown in Google Play) from the Me tab. After that no ads are shown and hints and second chances are free. You can restore the purchase on a new phone with "Restore purchase".

Please note

Never tap an ad by accident: tap the close button (X) when it appears. We do not control the content of ads. If you see an ad that is inappropriate, you can report it to Google using the AdChoices icon on the ad.
